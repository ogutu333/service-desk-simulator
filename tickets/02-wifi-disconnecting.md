# Ticket 02 — Wi-Fi Keeps Disconnecting Every Few Seconds

**Category:** Network / Wi-Fi
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Resolved
**Reported By:** Nina Patel
**Department:** Engineering
**Location:** Not specified
**Contact:** x6195

---

## Issue Summary

Nina Patel reported that her Wi-Fi connection was repeatedly disconnecting every few seconds.

The intermittent connection was affecting her ability to maintain a stable network connection.

### Business Impact

* Video call dropped three times
* Unable to maintain a stable connection
* Disruption to the user's work activities

---

## Initial Assessment

The repeated disconnections initially suggested a potential network connectivity issue.

I first needed to determine whether the problem was persistent or a temporary network interruption before taking further troubleshooting or escalation steps.

---

## Troubleshooting Process

### 1. Contacted the User

Asked Nina through **Company Chat** whether her Wi-Fi connection was still unstable.

This allowed me to determine whether the connectivity problem was ongoing or had recovered on its own.

### 2. Monitored the Reported Connectivity Issue

The connection stabilized on its own within approximately one minute.

Based on the behavior, the issue appeared to be an intermittent, self-healing network interruption rather than a persistent outage.

### 3. Confirmed Recovery With the User

Once Nina confirmed that her connection was back, I replied through **Company Chat** to confirm that the connection had recovered.

No reboot or ISP escalation was required.

---

## Findings

The Wi-Fi interruption was transient and resolved on its own within approximately one minute.

There was no indication that the user was experiencing a sustained connectivity outage that required additional remediation.

---

## Root Cause

The simulator identified the issue as an **intermittent, self-healing network blip** rather than an ISP outage.

A specific underlying technical cause was not identified.

---

## Resolution

The issue resolved without requiring a system reboot, network equipment restart, or ISP escalation.

I:

1. Contacted Nina through Company Chat.
2. Checked whether the connection was still unstable.
3. Observed that the connection stabilized on its own.
4. Confirmed the recovery with Nina through Company Chat.
5. Resolved the ticket after confirming that connectivity had returned.

**Final Status:** ✅ Resolved

---

## What I Learned

* Not every connectivity problem requires an immediate technical intervention.
* I learned to distinguish between a transient network interruption and a persistent connectivity issue.
* Confirming that the user's service has recovered is an important part of closing a service-desk ticket.
* Escalating or rebooting equipment unnecessarily can create additional disruption when an issue is already self-resolving.

---

## Security Considerations

Although this was primarily a connectivity issue, unstable network access can affect a user's ability to securely access company services.

When troubleshooting connectivity problems, it is important to avoid unnecessary changes to network configuration or security controls when the issue is temporary and has already resolved.

If the interruptions had continued, further investigation would have been appropriate to determine whether there was an endpoint, wireless network, or infrastructure-related problem.
